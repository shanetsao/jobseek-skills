## 8. QA log

A package is not ready until every item passes. Failed rendering or
unresolved checks leave the files as drafts.

- [ ] Grammar gate: dedicated proofreading pass over the final text of
      every document — grammar, punctuation, subject–verb agreement, tense
      consistency, articles, spelling — every error fixed.
- [ ] Page counts checked against the format rule (industry resume and
      cover letter).
- [ ] Every page rendered and inspected (judge by the PDF render, which is
      the reference render — not an in-app DOCX preview).
- [ ] Text extracted and checked: completeness, reading order, names,
      dates, links.
- [ ] PDF and DOCX agree.
- [ ] Every substantive claim checked against usable ledger facts
      (user-attested or confirmed only); no invented numbers, skills,
      publications or leadership claims.
