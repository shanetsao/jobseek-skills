# CV / Resume Format Standard — the "clean style"

The single consistent format for **every** CV/resume built in this workflow
(industry resumes, academic CVs, master CV). Content depth varies by type
(industry = 2 pages unless the employer specifies otherwise; academic and
master = full detail); the visual format below does not vary. The style is
benchmarked on the Harvard GSAS resume samples and the UPenn STEM postdoc
two-page sample.

**PDF is the reference render.** Judge every build by the PDF render or by
the downloaded file opened in Word — never by an in-app DOCX preview, which
may substitute fonts and compress tab stops. **Never invent a layout** for a
build: use this spec exactly.

## Spec

- Page: US Letter (8.5 × 11 in). Margins: 0.75 in left/right, 0.6 in
  top/bottom.
- Body font: Times New Roman 10.5 pt, line spacing 1.08. No tables, no
  graphics, single column, black only.
- Header (all centred):
  - Name: bold, 17 pt, regular case — "Your Name, Ph.D."
  - Contact line: 9.5 pt — City, ST | email | phone | LinkedIn URL
  - No tagline. No street address.
  - No Google Scholar listing or metrics on industry resumes.
- Section headings: bold, 10.5 pt, ALL CAPS, black rule beneath; ~9 pt
  space before, ~4 pt after.
- Role blocks (two lines): line 1 bold — title first, e.g.
  "Postdoctoral Fellow | PI: Dr. …"; line 2 — institution in italics, dates
  right-aligned on the same line (right tab stop at the text edge).
- Bullets: Word "List Bullet" style, STAR structure, quantified wherever
  the ledger supplies a number, ~3 pt space after, 0.25 in indent. Strong
  action verbs; no "responsible for".
- Core skills: grouped lines, each with a bold category label (for example
  "Cell therapy: ", "Assays & analysis: ", "Antibody engineering: " —
  labels tailored per resume), items separated by "; ".
- Education: institution bold with year right-aligned; degree in italics
  beneath.
- Publications, manuscripts, presentations, patents: hanging-indent
  paragraphs (0.25 in), journal names in italics — not bullets.
- Awards: one bullet per award.
- Industry section order: SUMMARY → CORE SKILLS → EXPERIENCE → EDUCATION →
  SELECTED PUBLICATIONS → MANUSCRIPTS IN PREPARATION → SELECTED
  PRESENTATIONS → PATENT → AWARDS → LEADERSHIP, MENTORING, AND TEACHING →
  REFERENCES (academic/master only).
- SUMMARY stays prose.
- No objective statement. No references on industry resumes.

## Why this style

The clean look comes from: (1) a serif face (Times New Roman) instead of a
sans face; (2) dates right-aligned on their own edge instead of inline
after a pipe; (3) title-first role lines with the institution in italics
beneath; (4) publications and presentations as hanging-indent paragraphs
with italic journal names, not bullets; (5) skills grouped under bold
category labels instead of one long separated line; (6) a quieter header
(regular-case name, no tagline); (7) more white space — wider margins and
airier spacing.

This format also matches the consensus in Harvard, UPenn, MIT, Stanford
BEAM and comparable PhD/postdoc guidance: 2 pages for PhD-level industry
roles; experience before education for postdocs; STAR / Skill–Context–
Outcome bullets, quantified; selected (not full) publications on industry
resumes; PI/lab named in role lines for research roles; no references and
no objective on a resume; ATS-safe (no tables, graphics or text boxes; JD
keywords carried in the text).

## Rejected improvements — do not apply

Five research-backed improvements were trialled on 2026-11-18 and then
**dropped** the same day after careful review. Do not apply any of them:

1. A page-2 footer (name + page number).
2. A "(n of N)" publications count in the Selected Publications heading.
3. The summary rewritten as bullets instead of prose.
4. Award selectivity annotations.
5. Folding the Manuscripts in Preparation section into Selected
   Publications.

The standard is the spec at the top of this file, unchanged.
