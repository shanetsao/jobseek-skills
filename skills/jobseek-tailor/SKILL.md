# Jobseek Tailor

Read [shared records](../jobseek/references/records.md), [state commands](../jobseek/references/state-commands.md), and the [tailoring workflow](../jobseek/references/tailoring.md).

1. Resolve the selected applicant and job. Recheck posting availability and JD changes. Inspect the current ledger and any prior feedback that actually applies to this job.
2. Select usable evidence and prepare a concise requirement-to-evidence mapping. Develop accomplishment bullets from supported STAR details; keep factual lists and citations conventional. Ask for missing results rather than fabricate them.
3. Follow employer instructions and applicant format preferences. Default industry resumes to two pages in a clean single-column layout unless the target employer specifies otherwise. Academic CVs have no fixed page cap.
4. Generate both PDF and editable DOCX from the same content. Use the available document/PDF workflows and bundled runtime; render and inspect every page and verify extracted text before presenting a submission-ready version.
5. Present the JD, both documents, evidence mapping, and unresolved questions for review. Keep internal evidence notes and drafting instructions out of submission documents.
6. Record feedback by type and scope. A factual correction updates the ledger with provenance; a wording rejection does not. Do not infer global preferences from a single rejection or from silence.
7. Record approval only after an explicit applicant decision and against the exact job/JD, document versions, source fact revisions, and answer set. Changed material returns to review. Never treat approval of wording as evidence for a new fact.

Use the answer bank only when its facts and scope remain current; adapt job-specific answers and obtain review. Do not populate sensitive or declarative responses from assumptions. This skill prepares materials; it does not send them to employers.
