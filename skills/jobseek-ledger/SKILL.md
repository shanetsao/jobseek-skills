# Jobseek Ledger

Read [shared records](../jobseek/references/records.md), [state commands](../jobseek/references/state-commands.md), and [onboarding and ledger workflow](../jobseek/references/onboarding.md).

1. Resolve the selected workspace and applicant profile before reading or writing applicant data. Inspect current records and revisions rather than relying on remembered history.
2. Process only the material supplied or identified for this task. Preserve source files separately and record source location, provenance, and review status. Use explicit user statements as user-attested facts; keep extracted facts pending until reviewed.
3. Capture detailed experiences and STAR stories without replacing uncertainty with a polished claim. Separate individual contributions from team results and experience from demonstrated proficiency.
4. Surface contradictions in dates, titles, outcomes, attribution, publication/patent status, and other consequential facts. Do not silently choose a version. Corrections preserve history and flag dependent application materials for review.
5. Ask focused questions that materially improve an active job search or missing accomplishment evidence. Do not require every possible ledger field to be populated before useful work begins.
6. Store fact updates, career preferences, wording feedback, and application choices in their respective records. A rejected bullet does not erase the underlying experience. Never infer why the applicant rejected an item.
7. Save through the shared helpers, validate state, and refresh the readable dossier only after reconciling manual edits. Summarize additions, conflicts, and questions that remain.

Store applicant records in the selected workspace, never in this skill package. Do not import personal details from unrelated profiles or conversations. No missing metric, outcome, qualification, or job preference may be guessed.
